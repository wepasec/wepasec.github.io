"I think all pop music should be about who can make the loudest, brightest thing," SOPHIE once said. "The challenge I'm interested in being part of is who can use current technology, current images and people, to make the brightest, most intense, engaging thing."

Join us for an all-night celebration of SOPHIE's 40th birthday, honoring Her life, music, and attitude as we chase the loudewst and brightest all night long.

L-L-Lemonade [caff. & decaf electrolyte drinks] will be freely provided.

Guiding the night's sonics will be:

**Enema**, opening up with a varied & SOPHIE-inspired dance set that will make you sweat.

**[LEFSE](https://soundcloud.com/zachcsv/live-flashpoint-8-7-26)** (PGH [hyperBOP!](https://www.instagram.com/_hyperbop_) resident), holding it down and sending you up the wall with a mix of techno & hyperpop.

**[angelgirl EVA](https://angelgirleva.net)** (CLE), with a hybrid live/DJ set showcasing her cutting edge electro pop defined by distorted vocals, atmospheric synths, and precision driven rhythms.

**[mooneyes](https://soundcloud.com/user-105775171)** (Erie), bringing a hard-hitting mix of hyperpop edits & more to get us higher before saying goodbye.
