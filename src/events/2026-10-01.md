[**The Body**](https://thebody.bandcamp.com/) brings their legendary, heavy, and innovative music to Pittsburgh. Their unmistakably singular sound is known for its monolithic force, inventive production, and deceptively nuanced arrangements. The Body has produced a wealth of groundbreaking collaborations and benchmark albums which have changed the perceptions and directions of heavy music.

Supporting is a can't-miss lineup of heavy hitters:

**Ritual Mass**, technical death metal quartet with a [new record](https://20buckspin.bandcamp.com/album/cascading-misery) out on the great 20 Buck Spin

[**Alamoans**](https://killenemyrecords.bandcamp.com/album/alamoans), local hardcore punk darlings, back after a long hiatus.

[**Some Pepper**](https://somepepper.bandcamp.com/), playing a splattering of techno/whatever heavy mashed up electronics.

... and **Dilettante**, the Pittsburgh heavy drone veteran playing tunes to open and drones throughout the evening.
