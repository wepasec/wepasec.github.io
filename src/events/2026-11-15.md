[Purelink](https://purelink.bandcamp.com/) is an ambient music trio from Chicago, United States. The group, comprised of Ben Paulson, Tommy Paslaski, and Akeem Asani, formed in 2020 and has been described as an "ambient boyband." 

With local support from [Davis Galvin](https://davisgalvin.bandcamp.com/) (live set), [Gusto](https://www.instagram.com/miscellaneous.records/) (live set), and No Hitter (Anthony Ambroso, David Bernabo, Brian Riordan, Eli Weidman).

DJs [m.Kim](https://soundcloud.com/rose-544165350) and [DJ Furniture](https://soundcloud.com/dj-furniture) in between acts.
