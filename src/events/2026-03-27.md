For our first official dance night we welcome 3 DJs who we've worked with closely in the past for a night of propulsive and pulsing techno to bop to until we... COLLAPSE

DJ Enema opens with crunchy electronics, hard hitting club cuts and high energy rave techno. Azrael will follow with their signature swanky and groovy techno, and Repeat Offender will close out the night, bringing us deeper into techno and hitherto unknown locales.

**STROBE WARNING** - there will be bright flashing lights at this event. Excercise caution if you or someone you know is sensitive.

This event is 21+ but we do not serve alcohol. You are welcome to bring your own beverages, in moderation.

Please be mindful of the space both indoors and out and keep volume low when hanging outside. Good relations with our neighbors are key to the longevity of the space.

As always water and earplugs will be plentiful and freely available.
