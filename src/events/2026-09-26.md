[stress tess](https://soundcloud.com/stresstess)

stress tess is a Brooklyn-based DJ exploring the hypnotic edges of techno and psychedelic dance music. Her long-form sets move patiently through shimmering minimalism, pulsing rhythms, and deep groove, creating immersive soundscapes that invite listeners inward. She is a founding member of [Vortex](https://www.instagram.com/vortex.bklyn/), the collective behind Area X, a New York party series devoted to cultivating intimate dance floors and expansive states of listening.

[dj33L](https://soundcloud.com/dj-33l)

dj33L is a DJ and organizer based in NYC. A product of Brooklyn’s DIY music scene, he seeks to contribute more ways in which we can hold space for each other and advance our shared journey of understanding and self discovery. dj33L is drawn to hypnotic and minimal techno as the foundation of his sound, also shaped by hardgroove and punk influences. This manifests as warmth, spaciousness, and intrigue in thoughtful narrative arcs. 

[Formosa](https://www.instagram.com/formosa.exe/)

Formosa is a multidisciplinary Taiwanese-American DJ, artist, and designer who performs as "formosa" and as half of regular Pittsburgh queer party and music duo, [Jellyfish](https://linktr.ee/jellyfishpgh). Behind the decks from Pittsburgh to New York, Toronto, London, Mexico, and Taipei, you can expect their sets to range from driving italo to electro, acid house, drum and bass, breakbeat, minimal, and more. Formosa will take you on a journey through driving bass lines and syncopated rhythms, sprinkled with playful melodies. You can catch their sets on SoundCloud, at any Jellyfish party, and at your local discotheque.
