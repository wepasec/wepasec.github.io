Delirium: lose your mind, reconnect with your body, rejuvenate your soul, enmesh with the cosmic. We imagine better futures while making space for collective escape and expression in the now, bringing communities together in the freeing ritual of dance and rhythm.

Delirium is queer divinity, trans transcendence, radical acceptance, open artistic exploration with foundations of care and love for the people.

For our inaugural event, we’re blessed to have NY’s acid queen [Scotia](https://www.instagram.com/scotia_trax) in Pittsburgh for 3 hours of blissful dancefloor heat, with support from local community builders and sonic alchemists [Lemonline](https://www.instagram.com/__lemonline/) ([Planet Lush](https://www.instagram.com/planet.lush/)) and [Rosy](https://www.instagram.com/rosy.makes.music/) ([Everdark](https://www.instagram.com/everdark_pgh/), [Kindling](https://www.instagram.com/kindling.pgh/)). Come early, stay late, dance hard ✨

In the tradition of the second summer of love, we practice “no alcohol, no attitude”; 
Our smart bar will provide non-intoxicating concoctions to keep you hydrated and dancing all night!

21+, BYOB permitted but discouraged, $20 earlybird // $25 online regular // $30 week-of-show, with a limited number of door tickets at the high tier. This goes to support all the incredible artists involved; marginalized folks who would be barred by the price, please get in touch :)
