[Cate Kennan](https://cargocollective.com/catekennan) is a composer, producer, and recording artist from Los Angeles. Her music unravels itself slowly as a misty vignette from a dream. Shadowy, distant, murky, and nocturne. Her [second full-length album](https://catekennan.bandcamp.com/album/shadows) was released in June on Kranky Records.

Additional nocturne sets will be provided by Bed Load & 7D, with interstitials by swatchdj.
