○ with trē, plasma arc, Amby Doosters, LMN, Cyberemesis, Tony Montgomery, and the CALLUNA band ○

_In Sunlight or in Shadow_ is the first release by [CALLUNA](https://www.instagram.com/ca11.11una), aka Heather Harper. Dropping 11.11.2026 on Pittsburgh's [Cleaner Tapes](https://cleanertapes.bandcamp.com/) label, _ISOIS_ is a solargraph, a seismic image received across a vast distance.

On 11.10.2026, join CALLUNA and her friends to celebrate the release, along with [trē](https://www.tresoundartist.com/), [plasma arc](https://plasma-arc.net/), [Amby Doosters](https://ambydoosters.bandcamp.com/), [LMN](https://www.instagram.com/lmn.aiff/), [Cyberemesis](https://www.instagram.com/cyberemesis/).

_In Sunlight or in Shadow_ will be played in full, with live video projections by CALLUNA, dance by [Tony Montgomery](https://www.instagram.com/tony.toni.toeknee/), and accompaniment by the CALLUNA band (drummer [Braden Ball](https://www.instagram.com/dang_clemfano_/) of Riften Rain and Calico Dogs, violinist Nikki Thomas, violist Thomas Scheurich, and trē on flute).

Flyer by [Deb JJ Lee](https://www.instagram.com/jdebbiel)
