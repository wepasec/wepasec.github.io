SEDIMENT is an accumulation and celebration of trans excellence in the world of electronic music,
featuring a mixed-genre lineup performing a wide variety of original music.

SEDIMENT is a testament to resilience in the face of erosion, a conglomeration of form, joy, hope, despair, and pain
all under one roof. We proudly boast an all transgender lineup, featuring the following artists from the
depths of the internet to right here in Pittsburgh:

[★ Colloid Wing ★](https://free99.net)

Bones don't measure the outside, they register load.
Burned, stripped, stump still alive.
Like a tenth—heartwood. 
Left foot always ahead of the right.
Dante as an Oak Tree.
Where the dog saint holds his own head, a stranger in his own body.
His skin wants the wound.
What survived the cutting—still singing as it floats downstream.

[★ E_DEATH ★](https://eternallife.bandcamp.com/)

[★ angelgirl EVA ★](https://angelgirleva.net/) is a music artist and DJ operating at the intersection of underground club culture and electronic pop. Working across production, live performance, and visual media, she constructs immersive sonic worlds defined by distorted vocals, atmospheric synths, and precision-driven rhythms. EVA's work explores identity, digital embodiment, and the tension between intimacy and spectacle in an era of constant exposure. Her output draws from IDM, witch house, hyperpop, and techno, balancing emotional vulnerability with physical intensity designed for both solitary listening and the dancefloor.

[★ Detatchi ★](https://linktr.ee/detatchi) is a Columbus, OH based producer and vocalist making hyperpop, emo, and digicore music for trans girls and furries. She is currently working on her debut album, centered on themes of rebellion and self-certainty. Musically, she takes inspiration from artists like UNderscores, 100 gecs, and Saoirse Dream with a bit of digital hardcore mixed in.

[★ Lexi Valentine ★](https://linktr.ee/LexxxiValentine) is a musician and producer who has been based in Pittsburgh for the last 2 years. Her style takes influence from the articulate, sample-heavy chops of French electronic music, as well as the eclectic energy and complex rhythms of the juke and footwork scenes. You can find her music easily online, or you can ask her for a CD if you’d like ★彡

[★ Rosy ★](https://rosymakesmusic.bandcamp.com/) (She/Fae) is a producer, DJ, and synthstress.
She's equally at home on experimental ambient, noise, live electronics, and dungeon synth shows as
a beat-masher and in the club mixing. She's a founding member of both Everdark and Kindling collectives.
Fusing breakbeats, experimental dance music, art music, and a love of video game sound, Rosy builds tracks that feel both familiar and unexpected. Her music embraces multiplicity - containing layers of history, queerness, and play that reward repeated listening. Fae has previously played at Club Pittsburgh and Hot Mass, as well as Bad Sauna, Poetry Lounge, Cattivo, Remedy, and dozens of renegades. She's a winner of the Rising Artist Foundation's Fourth River Grant (2025).

[★ Homeowners Association ★](https://homeownersassociation.neocities.org/)

Relinquished to whispers in chatrooms, duo Homeowners Association are transcribing primal fear
into sonics unrelenting and pure. Dance away what you were. Discretion advised.
