Seldom Seen : The Final Sandman 

Film Screening! 

*one night only*

Doors at 9pm / Films at 10pm

(special midnight theater release)

[Sandy Loaf](https://www.sandyloaf.com/) will be premiering her final film in the Sandman Series : 'Seldom Seen.' This will complete her first trilogy film series. For the first time you can watch the entire story unfold. She will be showing her film alongside / in collaboration with VFX by [Nasty Taxi](https://linktr.ee/nastytaxi) & Musical Performances by [Bella Figlia](https://www.bellafiglia777.com/) throughout the evening. 

Two short films will also be shown to start off the screening by artists / filmmakers : [EDENISTIX](https://www.instagram.com/edenistix/) (Pittsburgh) and [Guadalupe Arellanes](https://www.instagram.com/gwadalinaaa/) (Los Angeles) 

Sculptures will be present on site. Pittsburgh cookie table and refreshments included.
BYOB 21+

Come enjoy five powerful women making art together!
