VESPIDAE is an intricate web of resonance. It is humming, twitching, buzzing.
We entangle our ears and minds alike. At any given moment, a fleeting transient or
thunderous kick can change your perception of what music can be. From a nest of 
hardware and wires, to live-coded digital extremes, to music systems that, simply put,
create sounds only imaginable with today's raw computational power, we are here to
celebrate sonics and their gravity that binds us.
