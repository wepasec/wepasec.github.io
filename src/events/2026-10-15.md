What is a grindhouse?

A grindhouse theater takes its name from showing continuous films with low admission fees, grinding through reels of low budget, sleazy, and taboo B-movies.

Peaking in the 70s, grindhouses have evaded censorship, and screened exploitation films “too outrageous” for a studio theater, using shocking subjects like sex, drug use, crime, along with gore and splatter horror.
