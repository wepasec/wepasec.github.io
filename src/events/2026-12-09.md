[Jimmy Cicero](https://jimmycicero.com/), now on his ninth life, joins us from LA to lead an evening of dreamy, sentimental, hypnagogic post-Americana & indie. Jimmy's [debut release](https://jimmycicero777.bandcamp.com/) under his own name finds his seasoned baritone drifting through glowing analog arrangements-equal parts living room, cathedral, and late-night AM radio.

Locals [Westinghouse Atom Smasher](https://www.instagram.com/westinghouse.atom.smasher) bring their uniquely Appalachian blend of post-country / ambient Americana & folktronica to the evening. Their 2026 album ["Wish Signal"](https://westinghouseatomsmasher.bandcamp.com/album/wish-signal) showcased a mighty fine collection of songs, a worthy follow-up to 2024's excellent ["Pittsburgh, Transylvania"](https://westinghouseatomsmasher.bandcamp.com/album/pittsburgh-transylvania).

On opening duties is PGH dream pop band [Acid Drag](https://aciddrag.bandcamp.com/track/spider-song-demo). The evening will close out with a drone set by [Bed Load](https://timeandagain-eds.bandcamp.com/album/music-for-bed).
