w/ special guest Dr. Claire Cohen from [Doctors Against Genocide](https://doctorsagainstgenocide.org/) and [National Single Payer](https://nationalsinglepayer.com/)

A presentation and discussion of **Depose: Luigi Mangione, the Right to Health, and the Unfinished American Revolution**.

Written by the **December 4 Legal Committee**, the anonymous collective that created Luigi Mangione's legal defense fund, *Depose* takes the American healthcare debate onto the terrain of the fundamental political questions facing all of us today.

The presentation will be given by a spokesperson for the authors. Music to be performed by [Funeral Parade of Roses](https://soundcloud.com/funeral_parade_of_roses). Signed copies of book available for purchase. Presented by [Ill Will Editions](https://illwilleditions.com/). Learn more at [deposebook.com](https://www.deposebook.com)
