<p>Once upon a late nite bleary, while I wandered, fog so eerie,<br>
Over many a dull and fiending individual of brainless bore—<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;While I shivered, nearly flaking, suddenly there came a quaking,<br>
As of ev'ry one gently shaking, shaking ass at the warehouse door.<br>
"'tis One Whole," I marveled, "quaking at the warehouse door—<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;just One Hole and nothing more."</p>
