Joining us from Orlando, [Putrid Fauve](https://putridfauve.bandcamp.com/) brings a mindblowing showcase of noise performance art. Drawing from harsh noise, ambient, and industrial, it's work unfolds in a theater of the formless—conjuring primordial instinct, ritual violence, and sacred decay. Sound becomes a poetic gesture of violent imagery.

Supporting will be a fantastic cast; [Chien Maudit](https://chienmaudit.bandcamp.com/), (Putrid Fauve collaborator and fellow Orlando-native) and local legends [Negligible Wound](https://negligiblewound.bandcamp.com), [Farrah Faucet](https://linktr.ee/farrahfaucet), and [Some Pepper](https://somepepper.bandcamp.com/).

Flyer by [Bethany Wells](https://instagram.com/b.wells_was_here).
