A continuation of [Daia's](https://www.instagram.com/daiaart_/) 'Music with Friends' series- a musical improv night.
The theme of the night is sunflowers 🌻.
A twist for this iteration, it will be a little danceable, featuring [Buscrates](https://48hills.org/2023/07/the-indelible-hooks-and-dance-floor-iq-of-pittsburghs-buscrates/).
Expect some playing on music, building beats from scratch, and twisting around having fun.
[Steven Haines](https://www.instagram.com/fleamarketfilms/) will be on viz for the evening, with sunflower-themed moving images.
