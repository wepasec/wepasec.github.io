On October 30, All Hallow's Eve, we summon the dark and twisty baddies, the cuntiest club kids, the fab freaks, the spookiest sex sirens, *and* the weirdos, mister, to celebrate 9 years of The MoB. For 6 hours on one very wild, dark, haunting, and memorable as fuck night we open a portal to usher in our 10th year of operation as only we can. Starting at 10pm and well into demon time, we rage with some unrepentant killers:

HUNY XO

IDKRYAN (Houston)

Simisola (Brooklyn)

Queen Father

A SLAY. Let the bodies hit the floor. And we're not quite done: go-go dancers and hosts are still TBA.

Our gathering place, Collapse, is a DIY warehouse in Pittsburgh's East End. BYOB 21+. Water will be free and available all night, but plan for an early arrival to take advantage of a limited stock of cups, ice, and mixers. FYI while the venue is ground level, the bathrooms are not ADA accessible. The exact address and all further instructions will be mailed to all ticket holders at 3:00 PM Oct 30.

One again, this is THEE spooky kiki of the season here in PGH and your chance to solidify your place in MoB history. We hereby do summon thee to unleash on the dance floor. How many lives will you leave with after raving to the grave?

Mostbeautifullest (aka The MoB) is a Black queer party collective based in Pittsburgh, PA. Dubbed “the collective altering Pittsburgh’s nightlife scene for the better”, The MoB produces events from the perspective of advocation for and the centering of the QTBIPOC community. It’s giving kikis, club culture, and cuntiness. Every time.
