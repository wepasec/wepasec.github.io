Chicago-based hyperpop, hip-hop, and electronic artist **Fraxiom** will be performing as part of her *The Adventures in Question* tour, fresh off the release of her debut album [*The Adventures of Jester Jackalope*](https://fanlink.tv/TAOJJ). Fraxiom is also known for her collaborative work, including as half of the duo [Food House](https://open.spotify.com/artist/41f6TKH0EoBnj3N4xIp26g). Fraxiom's new album showcases, as [Ringtone Magazine](https://www.ringtonemag.com/articles/fraxiom-brings-standup-comedy-to-the-club-on-the-adventures-of-jester-jackalope) put it,
"a wicked mix of high energy hip-hop and dance music put through a hyperpop blender."

Supporting will be Columbus-based [**Detatchi**](https://linktr.ee/detatchi), self-described as a producer and vocalist making hyperpop, emo, and digicore music for trans girls and furries.

**Note**: Unlike most events at Collapse, this one is all-ages, and BYOB will not be permitted. The venue is not a bar and does not serve alcohol.
