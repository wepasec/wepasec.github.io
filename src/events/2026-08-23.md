Join us for a daytime celebration of the release of [Nomad Music](https://countyconservationdistrict.bandcamp.com/album/nomad-music),
the new album from County Conservation District. True to the nature of the highly collaborative album, the event will feature
performances by [Dittocrush](https://dittocrush.bandcamp.com/), Ten Cent Man, Holden Wolf (Baltimore),
[Virtua DX (Baltimore)](https://virtuadx.bandcamp.com/music), and (playing Nomad Music in full) Country Conservation District.

Part I of the extravaganza will take place at COLLAPSE. Free Pennsylvania Dutch Birch Beer will be provided. A donation of $5
is suggested for the event; possible either via the link below, or at the door.
