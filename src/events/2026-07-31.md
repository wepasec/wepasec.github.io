Three sides to a triangle - COLLAPSE, the Universal Observer, and YOU. 

Come help us complete **SHAPES** - the first edition of our Friday night dance series. 

3 DJs will be guiding us through the evening:

**DJ ENEMA** will handle opening duties, bringing an eclectic collection of dance club classics, trancey synth grooves and techno heaters.

Founder and organiser of Everdark collective, **Cereza** has been behind the decks at all your favorite DIY parties of recent years. Sharp yet soft, bouncy yet sublime, clangy and clamorous - Cereza fearlessly mixes a multicolored array of contrasting sounds across the pop, club and techno spectrum, whipping up a flurry of rave rapture and queer joy.

**DJ Treebug** will close out the night, drawing us deeper into the fold with a tapestry of psychedelic house, electro, and hypnotic techno. 

Expect the unexpected.

Tea and electrolytes will be freely provided.

♦ Join us and become one in ecstatic geometry ♦
