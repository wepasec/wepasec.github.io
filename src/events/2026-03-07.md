We/us brings you another nite of live PAs that will really get your heart pumping.

A name familiar to many, C_ROBO_ returns from LA to give this undisclosed location a proper live-coded thrashing. If you see someone in the crowd with a laptop bobbing their head alongside you… it's probably C_ROBO_.

CUBE brings from NYC his honed live set that lands somewhere between off kilter breaks / jungle, in-your-face techno, harsh noise, dynamic vocals and an occasional bit of serene ambient. Come to scream, come to shout but whatever you do, don’t stand still.

Then is the battle-hardened NONSITE. Having proven time and time again that the cross-section of techno, industrial, noise and minimal can charm us all. Many will feel right at home as that signature distorted kick drum starts driving the speakers.

Sitting as the bifurcating guardian between outer and inner state spaces, DOM MISJA opens the evening, granting passage through the liminal void. An experiment in spontaneous pulse field navigation with the Roland D-20 lays the mind fallow in preparation for the journey forward.

Come thru for some of it, come thru for all of it, but whatever you do, respect and look out for your fellow partygoers. Plus please respect the venue and its staff. We hope to see YOU on the dancefloor as we ring in this new space. 👁️
