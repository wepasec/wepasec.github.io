Three sides to a triangle - COLLAPSE, the Universal Observer, and YOU. 

We've rotated our usual Shape for a special Saturday treat:

Devoted to the evolutionary essence of Black dance music, [Jin & Juice](https://www.instagram.com/jinandjuic.e/) reads a room almost like she's had to her whole life; weaving club anthems through house and ghettotech with an intention that will hit every corner of our 4-point floor. If you caught her 2023 Honcho Campout set at the Grove, you already know what's coming.

[PVKVSV](https://www.instagram.com/pvkvsv/) [pah•kah'•sah] offers a rare live set to the void. Co-running Pittsburgh Open Decks and curating Brewery Sessions at Jackworth's Ginger Beer, PVKVSV is the kind of artist who's spent years holding space for others to experiment. Tonight it's his turn, and we have no idea what to expect.

[Gusto](https://www.instagram.com/gusto.misc/)'s sound is shaped by the underground house and techno he was raised on, and the reggaeton and dembow that surrounded him growing up Dominican-American in the Bronx. A resident DJ and booker at Hot Mass who also runs DETOUR and MISC, Gusto carries those roots into Pittsburgh's scene.
